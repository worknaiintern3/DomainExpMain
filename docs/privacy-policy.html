Privacy Policy for DomainPulse

Effective Date: October 7, 2026  |  Package: com.domainpulse.mobile

1. Introduction

Welcome to DomainPulse ("we," "our," or "us"). DomainPulse is a multi-cloud infrastructure, domain monitoring, and application telemetry platform developed to help developers, IT administrators, and digital teams monitor domain names, SSL certificates, cloud servers, and mobile applications from a single unified interface.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect, and handle your information when you use our mobile application (DomainPulse, package name com.domainpulse.mobile) and related backend services hosted at https://app.domainexp.info (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide and secure our infrastructure monitoring services:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, display name, and securely hashed password.
  • Infrastructure Configuration: Domain names, SSL certificate hostnames, DNS records, server hostnames, IP addresses, operating systems, and monitoring tags you add to your portfolio.
  • Provider Integration Credentials: Optional API tokens and keys (such as Hostinger, Cloudflare, AWS, DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Vultr, Linode, or GoDaddy) provided by you to enable live telemetry discovery.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Operating system version, device model, app version, network latency, and diagnostic crash logs.

3. Google Play Console & Google API Data

When you optionally connect your Google Play Console account via Google OAuth:

  • We retrieve your authorized Google Account email, Developer Account ID, and verified application package names with release track statuses.
  • We do not receive, store, or have access to your Google Account password.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

DomainPulse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell Google user data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train generalized AI/ML models.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for:

  • Monitoring domain expiration dates, SSL validity, and cloud server uptime.
  • Delivering timely push alerts before domains expire or services go offline.
  • Authenticating accounts and maintaining strict multi-tenant isolation.
  • Syncing and displaying authorized Google Play app statuses.

5. Data Security and Encryption

  • In Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and backend services is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • At Rest: Provider integration tokens, refresh tokens, and sensitive credentials are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Tenant Isolation: Customer data is strictly segregated so that no customer can ever access another customer's assets.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

We retain your account data only as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your data.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated telemetry directly inside the mobile app via Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Web / Email Request: You may request permanent deletion by emailing support@domainpulse.io or admin@domainexp.info with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  • Deletion Timeline: Upon verification, your account, stored credentials, and all monitored infrastructure assets are permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

DomainPulse is an enterprise and developer tool not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
